NOVA LITE

One-time setup so your phone talks to Nova privately (HTTPS) and can use its microphone.

  1. Get Nova's certificate.
    Download certificate

    iPhone: tap Allow, then open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on Alfred Local CA.
    Android: Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate.
    It only works for addresses on your home network, so it can't be used to fake real websites.

  2. Open Nova Lite.
    Open Nova Lite

    Checking the secure connection…

  3. Keep it on your Home Screen.

    In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen on the Nova Lite page.
